# CrewPay: video scripts

The Colosseum submission needs two videos: a **pitch** (2–3 minutes) and a **demo** (3 minutes or less). Record your screen with Loom or OBS, or Windows' built-in recorder (Win + Alt + R). Speak slowly; judges watch many of these.

---

## Pitch video (about 2:30)

You on camera, or slides with your voice. Each heading is one slide.

**1. The hook (0:00–0:20)**
**Replace this with a true story of your own**: a time you, or someone you know, worked in a group and pay went wrong. Judges remember a real story. If you don't have one, use:
"Every creative crew has the same story: we agreed on pay over WhatsApp, someone got paid late, someone got paid less than we said, and nobody could prove anything. CrewPay fixes that."

**2. The problem (0:20–0:50)**
"Music collabs, design teams, freelance crews: they work together on a handshake. Collaborators deliver and wait weeks. Leads pay deposits and people disappear. Nobody wrote down what 'done' means. And escrow tools are built for one buyer and one seller, not a whole crew."

**3. What CrewPay is (0:50–1:30)**
"With CrewPay, the Lead posts the deal: each role, a fixed price, an upfront deposit, and milestones with a clear 'done when'. Everyone accepts, counter-offers, or declines, and nothing is binding until everyone agrees.
Then the money is locked in a vault before anyone starts. Deposits go out instantly. Each milestone pays when it's approved, or automatically after seven days of silence. If there's a real disagreement, a neutral review splits the money, and the group chat is the evidence."

**4. Why Tempo (1:30–2:05)**
"We built this on Tempo because our users aren't crypto people. Every wallet is a passkey: Face ID, a fingerprint, or a phone. No app, no seed phrase. CrewPay pays every network fee, so a collaborator with zero dollars can sign a contract and get paid. The vault holds dollars, and every payout carries a payment reference. A whole project costs a third of a cent in fees."

**5. Where it goes (2:05–2:30)**
"Next we connect local money: crews pay in and cash out in naira, cedis or dollars through a partner like Yellow Card. Every finished project becomes a public work receipt, a track record you can't fake. CrewPay: agree on pay before the work starts, and get paid as it's done."

## Demo video (about 2:50)

Screen recording of **https://payclaw-six.vercel.app**. Prepare beforehand:
- Two browsers or windows: **Chrome** as the Lead, a **private window** (or Edge) as the collaborator. Both signed in, both with a wallet already created.
- One project already funded with a milestone already submitted, in case something is slow. Record the live steps first; cut to the prepared project only if needed.
- Hide your bookmarks bar, zoom the browser to 110%, close other tabs.

| Time | Screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Dashboard (Lead) | "This is CrewPay. I'm the Lead. Here's my wallet: it's a passkey, no app, no seed phrase." |
| 0:15 | New project | Fill in a name, a short brief, one role "Cover art", @collaborator, $100, 20% deposit, two milestones with "Done when". "Every milestone says exactly what done means. The checklist on the right won't let me send until the plan adds up to 100%." Click **Send invites**. |
| 0:50 | Collaborator window: the invite | "As the collaborator, I see my pay, my deposit, and every milestone. I could counter-offer. I'll accept." Tick the box, **Sign with my passkey**, approve the prompt. "That agreement is now on Tempo. CrewPay paid the fee." |
| 1:15 | Lead window: project page | "Everyone's signed, so I can fund." **Get test dollars** if needed, then **Fund project**, approve the passkey. "The money's in the vault, and the deposit already went out." Point at the vault card and the chat log. |
| 1:40 | Collaborator: milestone | Open the first milestone, add a note and a preview file, tick "This meets the Done when", **Submit for review**. "The Lead now has seven days. If they say nothing, I get paid anyway." |
| 2:05 | Lead: milestone | Play or open the preview. **Approve & release**. "Paid, instantly." |
| 2:20 | Collaborator: dashboard | Show the wallet balance going up. Click **Funded on Tempo ↗** or a transaction link: "Every step is on Tempo's explorer, and every payment is tagged with the project." |
| 2:35 | Project chat | "The whole history lives in the group chat. If there's ever a dispute, this is the evidence. That's CrewPay." |

**Tips**
- If a passkey prompt appears, approve it on camera. It's the moment that shows "no crypto needed".
- Don't read wallet addresses aloud. Point at them.
- Mention once that it's Tempo **testnet** with test dollars.
